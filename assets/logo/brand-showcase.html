Design Concept

logo-mark.svg

The Symbol

The abstract "S" monogram represents sleek through overlapping curved forms that evoke both a brush stroke and cascading design tokens.

The three-layer structure (outer stroke, inner fill, accent dot) symbolizes the layered nature of design systems — from broad color tokens to specific component styles.

The neon green accent (#00FF41) represents the AI-driven nature of sleek-ui — a modern, slightly futuristic touch that says "automation" without being overwhelming.

Typography

Inter was chosen for its clean, modern readability — perfect for a developer-focused tool that also serves designers.
